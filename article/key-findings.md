# JPEG Unplugged: key findings

Scope: Ethereum ERC-721 and ERC-1155 collections with at least 100 holders, not flagged as likely spam; uniform random sample of 3,000 of 49,494. Unit = collection. Last census result: 2026-10-22 02:21:24 UTC; headlines generated 04:36:59 UTC. Intervals are 95% Wilson unless stated.

**Top 100.** In CryptoSlam’s wash-filtered all-time Ethereum ranking, six collections have metadata and every sampled declared media field on-chain; 13 have dead or degraded delivery, 52 can be repointed by one key, and 49 depend on an ordinary web server. This separate famous-collection panel checks three token units per collection (306 overall), with two distinct IDs for 888 Inner Circle. Mooncats’ ranked wrapper returns empty metadata; this does not establish loss of the legacy art. 1. The universe is 58,617 collections with at least 100 holders; 49,494 fall outside the likely-spam flag. (`data/universe/collections.parquet`)
2. 5.1% (152 of 3,000; CI 4.3 to 5.9) have on-chain metadata; 55.2% use IPFS, 29.0% HTTP, 6.1% Arweave. (`data/analysis/headlines.json`: `random.metadata_location`)
3. 2.9% (87 of 3,000; CI 2.4 to 3.6) have metadata and all sampled media on-chain. This includes financial position/receipt NFTs and is an upper bound for rendering; only the gallery is render-tested. Of 152 with on-chain metadata, 63 (41.4%; CI 33.9 to 49.4) have at least one sampled media field off-chain: 60 through `image` (41 IPFS, four Arweave, 15 HTTP) and three only through `animation_url`. The remaining 89 include 87 candidates and two mixed-metadata collections. (`data/analysis/headlines.json`: `random.onchain.truly_onchain_census`, `fake_onchain_A_offchain_media_of_onchain_metadata`, `onchain_metadata_media_split`)
4. Metadata no longer loads for 30.3% (908 of 3,000; CI 28.6 to 31.9) under the legacy `dead` label: 20.9% direct negative evidence (627, derived as 707 minus 80 reverts), 2.7% contract reverts (80), 6.7% public-gateway misses (201). Another 6.0% are degraded and 2.7% unknown. Counting every unknown as unavailable gives 33.0%. Reverts and gateway misses do not establish permanent content loss. (`data/analysis/headlines.json`: `random.liveness`, `random.dead_reasons`)
5. DNS accounts for 39% and IPFS content not found for 31% of dead-or-degraded collections; gateway misses mean no tested public gateway served the CID within the observation window. (`data/analysis/headlines.json`: `random.dead_reasons.dead_or_degraded_share_of_failures`)
6. 19.7% (591 of 3,000) hard-code an HTTP gateway. Of the 147 using `ipfs.io`, 142 were unknown under the exact-URL check. Lighthouse refused the canary with HTTP 402 Payment Required; six collections use that paywalled gateway. Eight use the three discontinued Cloudflare/Infura gateway hostnames. (`data/analysis/headlines.json`: `random.exact_url`; `data/census/gateways.json`)
7. Metadata no longer loads for 34.5% of 2021 deployments and 37.6% of 2022 deployments, versus 15.4% for 2023. The 2026 broken-metadata anomaly consists of 32 `tokenURI` revert rows; gas limits and mint state remain unresolved, so it cannot establish rapid content loss. Deploy year is a mint-year proxy. (`data/analysis/headlines.json`: `random.by_year`; `data/census/census_collections.parquet`)
8. In the selected gallery, 71 of 83 tokens draw identically with the network blocked. A 2.8 MB archive of eight libraries cures 10 of the 11 failures; one uses the viewer's IP address. (`data/gallery/results.csv`; `data/dep-archive/MANIFEST.json`)
9. 16 of 474 Art Blocks projects (3.4%) reference a public-CDN library in generated HTML for one token per project. (`data/gallery/artblocks-survey/generator_survey.json`)
10. Of 160 reviewed project-authored storage claimants, 81 are repointable by one EOA or multisig account (75 EOA, six multisig); in the explicit subset, 53 of 103 (51 EOA, two multisig). A multisig can require several signatures. Storage outcomes: 131 holds, 11 on-chain-data/off-chain-pointer, zero contradicted, 18 unverifiable. Only `contradicted` means a failed claim. (`data/analysis/claims_summary.json`: `metrics`; `data/claims/claims_reviewed.csv`)
11. The historical 606-contract mutation audit has 302 with EOA pointer power, 23 with multisig pointer power, and 203 classed immutable. Its broader 323 EOA-mutable class includes 21 without EOA pointer power and is not a repointing count. (`data/analysis/claims_summary.json`: `metrics`; `docs/methods/mutability.md`)
12. Read-only checks found 204 cloud-resource candidates across 232 collections in pooled strata: 195 with missing-resource provider responses and nine with DNS-only evidence. Names may be re-creatable; reclaimability was not tested. (`data/hosts/hosts.csv`; `docs/methods/hosts.md`)
